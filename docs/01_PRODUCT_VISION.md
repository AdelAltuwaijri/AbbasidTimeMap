# Product Vision

## Working name
**Abbasid TimeMap**

## Vision
Create an immersive Arabic-first historical platform where users can **move through Abbasid history in time and space**, observe how the political and intellectual world changes, open events at their geographic locations, inspect historical evidence, and use AI to understand and reconstruct events without confusing generated content with verified history.

## Product statement
> A time-aware interactive map of Abbasid civilization that turns history from a sequence of articles into an explorable world.

## Target audiences
- General Arabic-speaking learners
- Students and teachers
- History enthusiasts
- Researchers needing a visual exploration layer
- Museums, cultural institutions, and education platforms in later phases

## Core user questions
The product must answer:
- What happened in this year?
- Where did it happen?
- Who was involved?
- What political entities existed at the time?
- Which scholars were alive and where were they active?
- Why did this event happen?
- What did it lead to?
- Which historical sources support the information?
- Is the information certain, approximate, or disputed?

## Differentiators
1. Time and map are synchronized.
2. Political boundaries are historical and time-dependent.
3. Events are connected to people, places, states, causes, impacts, and sources.
4. Historical uncertainty is first-class data.
5. AI explains and reconstructs from curated facts; it does not create the historical record.
6. The interface is Arabic-first with optional English support.
7. The platform is designed as an extensible history engine, with Abbasid history as the first corpus.

## Long-term vision
The same engine can later host:
- Umayyad history
- Al-Andalus
- Ottoman history
- Arabian Peninsula history
- Saudi history
- Comparative world timelines
